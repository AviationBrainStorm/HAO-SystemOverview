      Why this matters

      A HAO track is a regulatory problem only in combination with its altitude. The same ground position may sit inside ATC-managed airspace at FL550 and outside it at FL620, so the track here is coloured by flight level rather than by operator.

      Where HAO will require changes to existing ICAO provisions

      Severity is a 0–5 judgement of how far the requirement is from being met by an existing provision. Filter by the scenario selected in the explorer, or view all.

      Job cards, delivery dates and gap coverage

      Work programme elements use the ICAO Delivery / Effective / Applicability triple. Bars run from delivery to applicability where both are given.

      Gap coverage by the work programme

      Coverage is graded: resolving means a job card output would change the provision; partial means work exists but does not span the gap; framing means the gap is scoped conceptually but no provision changes as a result.
